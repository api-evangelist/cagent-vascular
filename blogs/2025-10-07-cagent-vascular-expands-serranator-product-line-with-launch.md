New larger diameters broaden treatment options for physicians managing complex peripheral artery disease Wayne, PA – October 7, 2025 – Cagent Vascular a leader in innovative endovascular technologies, today announced the commercial launch of the Serranator® PTA Serration Balloon Catheter in 7.0 mm and 8.0 mm diameters. This expansion brings the proven benefits of serration technology to larger vessels, including the iliac, SFA, and other challenging peripheral anatomies.
