# Cagent Vascular

> Cagent Vascular is an endovascular medical device company in Wayne, Pennsylvania that develops Serration Remodeling Therapy (SRT). Its Serranator PTA Serration Balloon Catheter embeds stainless-steel serrated strips in a semi-compliant angioplasty balloon to create controlled micro-fissures in the artery wall, treating peripheral artery disease (PAD) and chronic limb-threatening ischemia (CLTI). As of 2026-08-08 the company publishes no public API, developer portal, or machine-readable interface.
